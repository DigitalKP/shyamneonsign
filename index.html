What is the difference between LED neon and glass neon?

2nd-Gen silicone LED neon uses flexible LED strips mounted on 5mm clear acrylic. It is lighter, shatterproof, stays cool to the touch, runs on a safe 12V adapter, and consumes far less electricity than fragile glass neon tubing.
