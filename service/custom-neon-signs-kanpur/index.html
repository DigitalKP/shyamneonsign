Custom Neon Sign FAQs in Kanpur

How can I design my own custom neon sign in Kanpur?

Type your name, quote, or occasion text in the live customizer on this page, pick your font, neon colour, backboard cut, board size, and indoor or waterproof IP65 outdoor build, then tap Send Design on WhatsApp to get a workshop quote from Shyam Neon Sign.

What backboard styles do you offer for personalized neon signs?

Every custom neon sign is mounted on 5mm crystal-clear acrylic with your choice of cut-to-shape contour backing, clean rectangle backing, or matte black acrylic for extra contrast.

Can I order a custom neon sign in Hindi or English fonts?

Yes. We craft personalized LED neon signs in English cursive script, bold block, modern typography, and Hindi Devanagari calligraphy such as Shree Shyam, Radhe Radhe, or family surnames.

Are custom LED neon signs safe for bedrooms and kids rooms?

Yes. Our 2nd-Gen silicone LED neon signs contain zero fragile glass, stay cool to the touch, run silently on a safe 12V plug-and-play adapter, and can be paired with a brightness dimmer for bedroom use.
