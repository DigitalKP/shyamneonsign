What is a Diwali neon sign?

A Diwali neon sign is an LED neon sign made with a festive message or design, such as Shubh Deepawali or a Laxmi Ganesh name board. It gives the soft glow of neon tubing and lights up a home entrance, wall or shop front through the festival. At Shyam Neon Sign, each one is made from the text you choose.

Unlike fragile glass neon, our modern LED neon signs are lightweight, shock-resistant, low power-consuming (12V safe power adapter included), and last for years across every Diwali celebration.
